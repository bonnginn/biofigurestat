## Required reconstruction contract

Before a candidate becomes Tier G, create a versioned Gold Figure brief containing:

1. selected paper and exact panel;
2. one-sentence scientific message;
3. biological and displayed units;
4. factor cells, repeated/nested identity, and sample-size structure;
5. named primary contrast and comparison family;
6. control/reference/rescue roles;
7. descriptive versus inferential status;
8. X, series, facet, and hierarchical-label roles;
9. graph family, raw/summary layer, and error representation;
10. display, analysis, comparison, and annotation sets.

After the brief is fixed, compare it with historical synthetic raw:

- reuse raw only if units, factor cells, time spacing, pairing/nesting, readouts, and effect direction match;
- otherwise create a separate corrected context-rich version;
- never overwrite the historical benchmark case or its Round 1–3/Pool C evidence.

## Grammar coverage and known absences

The candidate set covers bars, grouped categorical layouts, multiple series/facets, paired data, repeated trajectories, distributions, nested raw + summary, proportions, Western blots, continuous/irregular X, survival, correlation, multiple readouts, and multiple-testing feature families.

The current evidence does not safely certify:

- descriptive-only Figure intent;
- rescue/reference comparison logic;
- control-vs-many or selected-comparison semantics;
- cross-sectional ordered time distinct from longitudinal time;
- dose-response/nonlinear fitting.

Do not add these grammars artificially. Add them only when source-backed reconstruction identifies appropriate cases.
